# Illusions of Intimacy

The full dataset is not publicly downloadable.

Published corpus statistics:
- 39,554 conversations
- 216,345 turns

Emotionally salient subset:
- 17,822 conversations
- 114,268 turns

The authors state that the full anonymized dataset is restricted
to approved research use under data-use agreements.

A public sample is referenced by the paper, but no complete
downloadable dataset is currently available to us.

Source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11649
